PhotoSlim Support

PhotoSlim finds your biggest photos and compresses them — up to 90% smaller — right on your iPhone or iPad. No accounts, no uploads, no subscriptions.

Getting started

  1. Open PhotoSlim and tap Allow Full Access when asked for photo access. This is required so the app can scan your library for large photos.
  2. If no photos appear, drag the size slider at the bottom to the left to lower the minimum size.
  3. Select one or more photos and tap Compress.
  4. Drag the slider in the compare view to see before/after and adjust quality, then tap Keep Both or Delete Originals.

Frequently asked

Are my photos uploaded anywhere? No. Everything happens on your device. Nothing is ever sent to us or any server.

Will I lose my originals? Only if you choose “Delete Originals.” Otherwise both copies are kept.

Does it keep dates and locations? Yes. Compressed copies preserve the original’s date, location, and album.
